# Organizations & Teams

Orchka is multi-tenant. Workflows, credentials, executions, and stats are
scoped to an **organization**. Every account belongs to at least one
organization — the **Personal** workspace we create for you on first
sign-in — and you can create more for separate teams, clients, or
environments.

## Concepts

| Concept | Description |
|---|---|
| **Organization** | The unit of data isolation. Has a name, a URL slug, and a set of members. |
| **Member** | A user with access to an organization. Has a role (owner, admin, or member). |
| **Active organization** | The org you're currently viewing. Stored on your session and updated when you switch. |
| **Invitation** | A pending member email. Expires after 7 days. |

Switching organizations re-scopes everything: the workflows list,
executions, credentials, and dashboard stats all narrow to that org's
data.

## The Organization Switcher

The switcher lives at the top of the sidebar.

- **Click your current org** to open the dropdown
- **Select another org** to switch — the page refreshes and queries re-scope
- **"New organization"** opens a dialog with a name and auto-slugified slug
- A checkmark marks the org you're currently in

```
┌──────────────────────────┐
│ ◆ Personal         ▾    │
└──────────────────────────┘
  Organizations
  ✓ Personal
    Acme
    Side Project
  ─────────────
  + New organization
```

## Roles

Every member has exactly one role within an organization:

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything — including renaming the org, removing other admins, and deleting the org. There can be more than one owner. |
| **Admin** | Invite members, change member roles (admin/member only), remove members, edit org details. Cannot delete the org or demote/remove owners. |
| **Member** | Use workflows, credentials, and execution data. Cannot manage other members. |

Roles are checked on the server: a member cannot bypass restrictions by
calling the API directly.

## Managing Your Team

Open **Settings → Team**. You'll see:

### Members

A table of everyone in the active organization, with their role chip.
Owners and admins see a **⋯** menu on every other member for role changes
and removal. Removing a member revokes their access to all workflows,
credentials, and executions in that org — re-invite them later if needed.

### Inviting

Click **Invite** in the page header. Pick:
- **Email** — where the invite link is sent
- **Role** — Member (default) or Admin

The recipient gets an invitation that's valid for 7 days. Until they
accept it, the invite appears in the **Pending Invites** section, where
admins can revoke it with one click.

### Organization Details (owner-only)

The bottom section lets owners:
- Rename the organization (the sidebar label updates immediately)
- Change the URL slug
- **Delete the organization** — permanently destroys every workflow,
  credential, execution, and stat rollup scoped to it. A two-step
  confirmation dialog guards this.

## Creating a New Organization

From any page:

1. Click the org switcher in the sidebar
2. Select **"New organization"**
3. Enter a name — the slug auto-generates as you type
4. **Create**

You're added as the owner and switched to it. The new org starts empty —
no workflows, no credentials, no executions.

## How Data Is Scoped

Internally every owned row carries an `organizationId` foreign key.
Every list query, create, update, and delete is filtered by your active
org via a middleware that reads `session.activeOrganizationId`.

This means:

- **Listing workflows** in Personal shows only Personal's workflows
- **Creating a credential** in Acme stores it under Acme's org
- **Switching to Side Project** instantly hides everything from the
  other orgs without re-fetching anything stale

If your session has no active org set (e.g. you just signed in), the
middleware falls back to your oldest membership.

## Existing Users: Backfill Behavior

Accounts that existed before organizations launched were each given a
**Personal** org as part of the rollout. All of your previous workflows,
credentials, and executions were assigned to that Personal org and your
session was set to use it as the active org. Nothing was lost — the data
is exactly where it was, just owned by Personal instead of you directly.

## Limits

- **10 organizations per user** (you can create at most ten orgs as
  owner; you can be a member of more than ten via invitations)
- **50 members per organization**
- **7-day invitation TTL**

These caps are configured in the Better Auth plugin and can be raised
for self-hosted deployments.
